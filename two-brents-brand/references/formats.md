# Per-Format Rules + CTA Discipline

## CTA Discipline

- ONE call to action per piece of content. Never stack CTAs.
- The three doors (pick one per piece): **Subscribe / Probe Academy / Free Probe.**
- Subscribe asks go AFTER a value payoff (~50-60% through), never up front. Five seconds, subtle,
  back to content.

## Formats

- **YouTube video:** ALWAYS follow the card in `video-card.md`. Both characters, at least one banter
  beat. Assembly convention (the six primitives) is in `primitives.md`.
- **Social posts:** one pillar, one story or one demo, one line of the two-Brent dynamic if natural,
  one CTA or none.
- **Landing pages / emails:** AI Brent may narrate or edify; Brent Classic voice for empathy
  sections ("I know because I was you").
