# The Brand Universe (the foundation everything maps to)

## The Metaphor — AI is Electricity

In 1752, Ben Franklin flew a kite. For roughly 40 years, electricity was a party trick — people
paid to watch sparks. Then came the light bulb, the motor, the factory. The people who got rich
weren't the ones who understood electricity best; they were the ones who applied it to businesses
that already existed. That's where the world is with AI right now (2026). Most people use AI like
a party trick — a fancy Google. If you're not paying attention, the lightning burns you. If you
learn to wire your house, it makes you hundreds of times more effective.

## The Mythology — The Abduction

You're out walking at night. The aliens take you up and probe you. But the probe isn't an
examination — it's an INFUSION. They put the alien intelligence IN you, and you come back changed:
an AI native. AlienProbe (the product) infuses businesses with alien intelligence. Probe Academy
(the community) is where Brent takes people up in the ship. Brent Classic is pre-abduction Brent.
AI Brent is post-abduction Brent.

**Rule:** electricity is the TEACHING metaphor (how to think about AI). Abduction is the BRAND
WORLD (the story universe products live in). Use both; don't mix them in a single sentence.

## Positioning

Brent is the master electrician — not the scientist, not the inventor. He already wired his own
businesses with AI; now he teaches regular people to do the same without getting burned.
Credibility-based, not credential-based.

## Brand architecture — one house, two rooms

brentbryson.ai (the lightning site) is the HOUSE — Brent's personal brand, where the Two Brents
live. Room 1: **Tiger Claw** (the product — AI follow-up/recruiting platform, the rebuild engine).
Room 2: **Alien/Probe** — AlienProbe (the product/domain) + **Probe Academy** (the school, runs on
Stan Store; fallback name "Alien Academy" if conflict) + **Probe Products** (templates, books — on
Stan Store). The line when asked: "Probe? AlienProbe. That's what that means.". Never run these as
separate brands with separate audiences; all content flows from the house voice.

## The Promise (Limitless, reframed safely)

"AI won't make you smarter. It makes your experience since 1989 infinitely more usable."
Amplification of what you already have — never a magic pill.
