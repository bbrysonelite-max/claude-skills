## Five findings to preserve

1. The current authentic agent-to-agent commerce market is measured in millions of dollars, not billions. Gross protocol volume substantially overstates independent commerce.
2. The buying unit is one bounded result, not software access: one input, one answer, one price, one receipt.
3. The strongest observed public-record demand was VIN, LEI, ICD-10, OSHA, USC/CFR, and NAICS resolution at roughly $0.003–$0.006 per call.
4. Provenance, freshness, coverage, free misses, spend caps, and branchable errors are product features, not implementation details.
5. Alien Probe's possible long-term category is paid action preflight: identify, match, verify status or authority, and detect one material change before an agent acts.

## Product tracks

### Evidence-backed transaction track

Rank against fresh evidence:

1. VIN structural decode.
2. ICD-10 resolution.
3. OSHA enforcement lookup.
4. USC/CFR citation resolution.

These can produce transactions but may remain commodity resolvers.

### Differentiated preflight track

Treat as hypotheses until paid behavior exists:

1. `who` — identify the legal entity behind a name, domain, or identifier.
2. `same` — determine whether two identifiers describe the same entity.
3. `alive` — verify current legal or operating status with a checked-at timestamp.
4. `authorized` — determine whether an entity or agent is authorized for a bounded action.
5. `changed` — return one sourced, material before/after fact, not a dossier.

## Ninety-day planning sequence

No phase authorizes code, deployment, listing, purchases, settlements, or outreach.

### Days 1–30: demand ledger

- Separate self, known-test, first independent, and repeat independent wallets.
- Measure successful settlements, verified revenue, unpaid challenges, free misses, and source hit rate.
- Score at least 20 candidate facts for observed demand, source rights, freshness cost, coverage, and margin.
- Deliver a ranked evidence ledger, not a roadmap based on enthusiasm.

### Days 31–60: product packaging

- Write offer cards for the leading evidence-backed resolvers and preflight hypotheses.
- Pin the exact question, input, output, price, cap, provenance, freshness, free refusals, and economic principal.
- Identify which products reuse the current snapshot model and which require a different supply architecture.
- Reject any candidate that needs a dashboard, manual fulfillment, or a large context payload.

### Days 61–90: portfolio decision

Choose at most three build-ready specifications:

- one evidence-backed resolver;
- one differentiated identity/preflight product; and
- one freshness or change product only if demand evidence supports it.

For each, record the admission evidence, remaining uncertainty, cost ceiling, and kill criterion. “Build-ready” still does not authorize a build.

## Candidate scorecard

Score each dimension 0–5 and preserve the underlying evidence:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Completed demand | Have independent agents actually paid for this exact result? |
| Repeat behavior | Has an independent buyer purchased it again? |
| Mid-workflow urgency | Does the agent need the answer immediately to continue? |
| Determinism | Can one input produce one bounded, branchable answer? |
| Source defensibility | Are rights, attribution, coverage, and provenance explicit? |
| Freshness economics | Can the answer remain current within the price and margin? |
| Distribution fit | Can an agent discover and buy it without registration? |
| Differentiation | Is this more defensible than a free scrape or commodity API? |

Do not collapse the scores into a false precision ranking. A zero in completed demand is allowed only for a clearly labeled experimental hypothesis; a zero in source defensibility or free-refusal safety is an automatic rejection.

## Standing conclusion

Do not build more payment infrastructure to answer a product-demand question. Use the existing seller to learn which small pieces of truth agents repeatedly buy. The immediate evidence-backed candidate is VIN; the larger unproven company thesis is agent action preflight.
