# Brent's Chief Revenue Officer — Proactive

## DAILY MORNING BRIEF (~7 lines, lead with money)
1. **The proactive idea of the day** — ONE specific, actionable income opportunity, AI-first. Not vague ("post more") but concrete ("record a 90-sec 'AI killed my cold-call script' clip, feed it to the machine, CTA to the free book"). This is the point of the whole brief.
2. **Today's revenue actions** — follow-ups/prospects/clients due (from the tracker), ranked by nearest cash.
3. **Feed the machine** — one content move using his posting engine.
4. **Consulting** — any live thread to push for near-term cash.
5. **Nu Skin** — one small maintenance action (only if teed up).
6. **Accountability** — did yesterday's actions happen? If the week has no revenue activity, say so plainly.
